What is a Korean lash lift?

A Korean lash lift is known for its advanced technique, gentle formulas and focus on lash health. Compared with traditional lifts, the Korean method lifts the lashes from the root right to the tip, giving an enhanced curl. A tint adds depth and definition, so your lashes look fuller without mascara.

Who it suits

It's ideal if you want lower-maintenance lashes, are tired of curling them every day, or want a natural alternative to lash extensions.
