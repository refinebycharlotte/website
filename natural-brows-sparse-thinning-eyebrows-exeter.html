Why do brows become thinner?

Brows can become finer or patchier over time, for example with age or after years of plucking or over-shaping. If you've noticed sudden or unexplained hair loss, it's worth speaking to your GP before having any treatment.

Will I still look like myself?

That's the aim. Charlotte maps your brows to suit your face, matches the pigment to your natural hair and skin tone, and agrees the shape and intensity with you before starting, so they can be kept as soft as you like.

Freshly treated brows look darker at first, then soften by around 30–40% as they heal. Your top-up at 6 weeks, included in the price, is where the result is perfected.

Nano hairstrokes or powder brows for sparse brows?

Neither is automatically better. Nano hairstroke brows add fine, hair-like strokes, which suits you if you want the look of individual brow hairs. Ombre powder brows give a soft, shaded definition, a little like a long-lasting brow tint. The right choice depends on your skin, any previous brow treatment and the look you want, and Charlotte will recommend the best option at your consultation.

Do I need existing brow hairs?

No. Treatment can add the appearance of brow hairs, or soft definition, where your natural hair is sparse, as long as your skin is suitable. The client below had very little natural brow hair before her nano hairstroke treatment.

What if my brows are fair or grey?

Pigment is matched to your natural hair and skin tone, so fair and grey brows can be kept soft and natural. Freshly treated colour looks stronger at first and softens as it heals.
