What are nano brows?

Nano brows are a precise machine technique that creates ultra-fine, hair-like strokes to replicate natural eyebrow hairs. They're ideal if you want to enhance your shape, fill sparse areas and create naturally fuller brows that don't look drawn on.

Nano brows or microblading?

Many people searching for microblading are looking for exactly this fine-hair effect. Charlotte doesn't use a manual blade; nano brows create the strokes with a precision machine, which allows bespoke results and is gentler on the skin.

Prefer a softer, shaded look? See ombre powder brows.

How it works

  1. Free consultation. We discuss your brow goals and preferred look, complete a medical review and carry out a patch test before treatment.
  2. Bespoke treatment. Your brows are mapped and shaped to suit your facial structure, and we fine-tune the outline together until you are 100% happy before starting.
  3. Top-up at 6 weeks. Included in the price. We check how your skin has held the pigment and adjust colour or shape so your result is even and long-lasting.
